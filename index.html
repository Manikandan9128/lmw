To our customers

Our machines are bought once and worked for decades — spinning lines, machining centres, castings in service. We engineer and support them for that whole life, not for the warranty period.

A family engineering house that has kept its word since 1962.

LMW Group is more than a machinery manufacturer - it is an engineering ecosystem. Founded in 1962 in Coimbatore, India, LMW has grown into a diversified engineering group with revenues exceeding USD 500 million and a presence across the world's major manufacturing markets. Behind it stands a structure few can claim: four core divisions - Textile Machinery, Machine Tools, Foundry, and the Advanced Technology Centre - surrounded by twelve group companies supplying castings, motors, precision parts, logistics and skilled talent.

Zero long term debt since inception.

Every critical input is engineered within the group - the same precision, mastered once, applied everywhere. A model built patiently over six decades.

Health
